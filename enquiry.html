Payment Policy

Proof of payment must be submitted before sessions begin. Payments are non-refundable once sessions have started.

Our pricing structure is designed to be flexible and accessible to students from different financial backgrounds. We aim to provide high-quality education support at reasonable and competitive rates.

Students can upgrade or change their plans depending on their academic needs and availability. We also offer guidance in choosing the most suitable plan to ensure maximum benefit.

Investing in your education is one of the most important decisions you can make. Our plans are structured to deliver value through consistent academic improvement and professional guidance.
